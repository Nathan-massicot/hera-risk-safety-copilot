# PECAN (France) — early access reimbursement for digital devices

## Why this concerns you (purpose)
PECAN ("prise en charge anticipée numérique") is France's **early-access** reimbursement
route for digital medical devices: it lets statutory health insurance pay for your app
**before** the full common-law listing, on a **presumption of innovation**, while you
generate the clinical evidence. It is the French counterpart to Germany's DiGA. CE
marking authorises the market; PECAN is what gets you **paid by Assurance Maladie** for a
**one-year, non-renewable** window — your runway to prove benefit and convert to the
permanent LPP/LATM listing.

## When it is triggered (scope)
Relevant when you seek **early French reimbursement** (Decree **no. 2023-232 of 30 March
2023**, under **Art. L162-1-23 SSC**) for a digital medical device that is either:
- **therapeutic (DTx)** → targets listing on the **LPP** (LPPR); or
- a **medical télésurveillance** device → targets the **LATM** (remote-monitoring list).

It is **not** for non-medical wellness apps, and the early window is **one year,
non-renewable** — you must transition to the common-law pathway before it ends, or
coverage stops. An app that cannot produce credible clinical evidence in 12 months is a
poor PECAN fit.

## Concrete obligations
- **Dual filing** — two parallel dossiers:
  - **ANS certificate of conformity** on **interoperability + security** (submitted via
    the **CONVERGENCE** platform);
  - **HAS clinical dossier** assessed by the **CNEDiMTS** (submitted via the **Sesame**
    platform), establishing the presumption of innovation/benefit.
- **Tariff** (Order of **22 April 2024**, per patient): a flat **EUR 435 incl. tax for
  the first 3 months** (single instalment), then **EUR 38.30/month** from the 4th month,
  with a **cap of EUR 780/year/patient**.
- **Interoperability/security**: meet ANS frameworks (couples with Ségur/DMP — see
  [[national_ehealth]]) and host French health data with an HDS-certified host (see
  [[hds_certification]]).
- **Evidence during the year**: run the study committed in the dossier so you can apply
  for permanent LPP/LATM listing before the 12 months elapse.

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm your device is a CE-marked DTx (→ LPP) or télésurveillance device (→ LATM).
- [ ] Prepare the ANS interoperability + security conformity dossier (CONVERGENCE).
- [ ] Prepare the HAS/CNEDiMTS clinical dossier (Sesame) for the presumption of innovation.
- [ ] Budget against the tariff: EUR 435 first 3 months, then EUR 38.30/month, cap EUR 780/yr/patient.
- [ ] Plan the evidence-generation study to transition to permanent LPP/LATM within the 1-year window.

## Examples (mHealth)
- A CE-marked DTx for chronic-pain therapy files for PECAN → ANS conformity + HAS
  dossier; reimbursed at EUR 435 then EUR 38.30/month while it runs its confirmatory study.
- A heart-failure **télésurveillance** app targets the **LATM** route under PECAN, then
  converts to permanent télésurveillance listing once benefit is shown.
- A device whose publisher hopes to **stay** on PECAN beyond a year → not possible: the
  one-year access is **non-renewable**; without the permanent listing, coverage ends.
