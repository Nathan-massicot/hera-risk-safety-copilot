# ISO/IEC 82304-2 — health software quality & safety

## Why this concerns you (purpose)
This is the **fallback for apps that are *not* medical devices**. If your app has **no
medical purpose** — a wellness, fitness, lifestyle or general health-information app —
it escapes the MDR/IVDR device regime (no [[mdr_class_iia]], no [[ivdr_class]]), but it
is **not unregulated**. The IEC/ISO 82304 family lets you demonstrate **quality, safety
and reliability** voluntarily, and earn a recognised **health-app quality label** that
buyers, insurers and app-store curators increasingly ask for. It is how you prove "we are
serious, even though we are not a medical device."

## When it is triggered (scope)
Relevant when your software is **health-related but does not meet the medical-device
definition** — i.e. the decision tree concluded "wellness" (no medical purpose) or
"observational tracking" (not SaMD). Two complementary standards apply:
- **IEC 82304-1:2016** — general **product-safety** requirements for health software
  placed on the market **without dedicated hardware** (covers the full lifecycle:
  design, validation, installation, maintenance, decommissioning).
- **ISO/TS 82304-2:2021** — **quality & reliability of health and wellness apps**,
  defining a scored **health-app quality label** (a "traffic-light" style label) used by
  manufacturers and independent app-assessment organisations.

If your app *does* have a medical purpose, it is **out of scope here** and falls under
MDR/IVDR instead — re-check via the SaMD/Rule 11 path.

## Concrete obligations
These are **voluntary** (no CE mark), but to claim conformity / obtain the label you:
- Define the **intended use, intended users and use environment** of the health software.
- Apply a **product-safety lifecycle** (IEC 82304-1) and software practices consistent
  with the [[samd_standards]] family (risk thinking from ISO 14971, lifecycle hygiene
  from IEC 62304) even though you are not a device.
- Address **ISO/TS 82304-2 quality dimensions**: healthy & safe, easy to use, secure
  data/privacy, robust/reliable, and effective (evidence of benefit).
- Undergo (optionally) a **third-party assessment** to earn the quality label.
- Remember the **horizontal laws still apply** regardless of device status: GDPR/Art. 9,
  ePrivacy, AI Act transparency, accessibility — being "non-medical" does not exempt you.

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm and document that the app has **no medical purpose** (so MDR/IVDR do not apply).
- [ ] Define intended use, users and operating environment per IEC 82304-1.
- [ ] Map your app against the ISO/TS 82304-2 quality dimensions and self-score.
- [ ] Decide whether to pursue an independent quality-label assessment.
- [ ] Verify horizontal obligations (privacy, security, accessibility) are still met.

## Examples (mHealth)
- A **step-counter / general fitness** app with motivational tips → wellness software;
  ISO/TS 82304-2 label is the right quality signal, not MDR.
- A **menstrual-cycle tracker** that only logs and predicts dates without any diagnostic
  claim → observational tracking → 82304 family rather than [[mdr_class_iia]].
- **Edge/negative:** a "wellness" mood app that **screens for depression and advises
  clinical action** has crossed into a **medical purpose** → it is **no longer** in 82304
  scope and must be qualified as SaMD ([[mdr_class_iia]] or higher).
