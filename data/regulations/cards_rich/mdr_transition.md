# MDR transition for legacy devices (Regulation (EU) 2023/607)

## Why this concerns you (purpose)
If your SaMD already holds a certificate under the **old directive (MDD/AIMD)**,
you are not yet under the full MDR — but you are living on borrowed time. Regulation
(EU) 2023/607 **extended** the MDR transition so legacy devices can stay on the
market while manufacturers complete MDR certification, **provided strict conditions
were met by mid-2024**. Miss those conditions and your legacy certificate's cover
collapses; meet them and you have until 2027/2028. This card tells you which clock
you are on.

## When it is triggered (scope)
Triggered when:
- your device/SaMD is a **legacy device** with a **valid MDD/AIMD certificate** (or
  a Class I device that was self-declared under the MDD but is up-classified under
  the MDR), and
- you intend to **keep it on the EU market** during the transition rather than
  certify fresh under the MDR.

A **brand-new SaMD certified today** applies the MDR directly and is **out of scope**
of this regime ([[mdr_class_iia]], [[mdr_class_iib]]).

## Concrete obligations
- **Extended deadlines** (amended MDR Art. 120):
  - **31 December 2027** — Class III and implantable Class IIb devices;
  - **31 December 2028** — other Class IIb, Class IIa, and Class I devices placed
    under a notified body (e.g. up-classified or sterile/measuring Class I).
- **Conditions to benefit** (all required): the device still complies with the
  MDD/AIMD and has no significant changes in design/intended purpose; it presents no
  unacceptable risk; the manufacturer had an **MDR-compliant QMS by 26 May 2024**;
  lodged a formal MDR application; and signed a **written agreement with a notified
  body by 26 September 2024**.
- The **"sell-off" period was removed**: compliant legacy devices already on the
  market may be made available **without a time limit**.
- Continue MDR-style **post-market surveillance and vigilance** in the meantime
  ([[pms_lifecycle]]).

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm the device had an MDR-compliant QMS in place by 26 May 2024.
- [ ] Confirm a signed notified-body agreement existed by 26 September 2024.
- [ ] Verify no "significant change" has been made to design or intended purpose.
- [ ] Map your device class to the right deadline (2027 vs 2028).
- [ ] Run the MDR certification project to finish before that deadline.

## Examples (mHealth)
- A Class IIb radiology-triage SaMD with a valid MDD certificate and a 2024 NB
  agreement -> may stay on the market until **31 December 2027**, then needs an MDR
  certificate ([[mdr_class_iib]]).
- A Class IIa medication-adherence app that had its MDR QMS and NB agreement in
  place in time -> covered until **31 December 2028**.
- A legacy app whose manufacturer **never signed an NB agreement by 26 Sep 2024**
  -> does **not** benefit from the extension; its legacy cover has lapsed and it
  cannot rely on these deadlines.
