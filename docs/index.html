Project goal. HERA Copilot helps teams building a digital-health (mHealth) application figure out early and clearly which regulations apply to them — a fragmented landscape spanning medical devices (MDR/IVDR), AI (AI Act), data protection (GDPR / Swiss nFADP), cybersecurity, market access and national reimbursement. This decision tree is its first building block: by answering a series of yes/no questions, the user watches the applicable obligations stack up, each documented by a detailed card (purpose, trigger, concrete obligations, checklist, mHealth examples and official sources).
